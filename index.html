WHAT IS LIGHT POLLUTION?

Light pollution is the excessive, misdirected, or invasive use of artificial outdoor lighting that alters natural outdoor light levels and negatively impacts the entire environment. It derives from streetlights, buildings, billboards, or other sources and can brighten the sky. This makes it harder to see stars and other celestial objects.

WHY IT MATTERS

Learn More

Light pollution reduces visibility of the night sky and can harm wildlife and human health. Small, easy changes to lighting can make a large difference.
