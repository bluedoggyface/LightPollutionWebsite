SAVE THE NIGHT SKY

Light pollution is the excessive or poorly directed artificial light that affects the night sky, the environment and human health.

Why Does it Matter?

Light pollution has several negative impacts:

  • It reduces the visibility of stars and constellations, decreasing our connection to the night sky.
  • It disrupts the natural behavior of nocturnal animals, affecting their feeding, reproduction, and migration patterns.
  • It can result in sleep disorders and other health issues in humans due to disrupted circadian rhythms.

The Problem

80% of people live under light-polluted skies

Thousands of stars have become invisible to the naked human eye
